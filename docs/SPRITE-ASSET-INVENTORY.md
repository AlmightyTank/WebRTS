## Art rules
- Consistent simple, low-poly-inspired, stylized modern military appearance.
- Terrain uses 2:1 isometric diamonds at 128×64.
- Decorations and buildings use transparent backgrounds and a ground-contact pivot for Y sorting.
- Keep source artwork separate from exported Godot-ready PNGs.
- Do not mark assets complete until actual files exist and are visually checked in Godot.
